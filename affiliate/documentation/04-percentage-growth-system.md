# 4. Percentage Growth System (Merit-Based)

Affiliates can increase their earning percentage through:

- education  
- contribution  
- leadership  
- consistency  

**Important:** Percentages only **activate** when the affiliate has **at least one direct member**. Before that, bonuses remain **stored** (not financially active).

Growth comes from three categories:

---

## 4.1 Education Bonus – up to +3.5%

Each completed **Sinegual Academy** course gives:

- **+0.5%**

There are **7 courses** → maximum **+3.5%**.

- Stored until the affiliate has directs.  
- Activated once they have at least one direct.

---

## 4.2 Community Support Bonus – up to +5% (0.5% per month)

Affiliates can earn additional percentage by helping others.

### Weekly Support Report (Mandatory)

Every member must declare **weekly**:

- who helped them  
- how they were helped  
- the quality of the help (1–10)  
- what value was created  

Each help action must be:

- **verified by the receiver**  
- **recorded in the system**  

### Monthly Growth Cap

Affiliates can gain:

- **maximum +0.5% per month**  
- **up to +5% total**  

This avoids burnout and keeps the system balanced.

---

## 4.3 Special Bonus – +1.5%

Awarded for:

- leadership  
- consistency  
- positive energy  
- zero-drama behavior  
- completing all monthly tasks  
- long-term reliability  

**Maximum:** +1.5%

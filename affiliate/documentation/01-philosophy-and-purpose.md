# 1. Philosophy & Purpose

The Sinegual Affiliate Program is **merit-based**, **ethical**, and designed to create **leaders**, not passive earners.

---

## It is NOT

- a multi-level marketing system  
- a “bring people and disappear” structure  
- a full-time job  
- an income farm  

---

## It IS

- a **personal-growth driven ecosystem**  
- based on **leadership**, **responsibility**, and **education**  
- designed to build **“mini-CEOs”** who manage their own small teams  
- inspired by **Warren Buffett’s philosophy** of managing people like companies

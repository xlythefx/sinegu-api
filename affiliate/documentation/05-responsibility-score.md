# 5. Responsibility Score (Portfolio Leadership)

Affiliates are **responsible for the people they bring**. Their “team” is their **human portfolio**, like Buffett overseeing his companies.

The **Responsibility Score** is influenced by:

- activity of their directs  
- growth of their directs  
- contribution of their directs  
- absence of drama or toxicity  
- completion of tasks by their directs  

- **High score** → easier to earn monthly growth  
- **Low score** → no growth awarded  

This ensures **quality over quantity**.

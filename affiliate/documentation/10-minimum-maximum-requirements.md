# 10. Minimum & Maximum Monthly Requirements

To prevent overworking and burnout:

---

## 10.1 Minimum Monthly Requirements (MMR)

Affiliate must complete **at least one** of:

1. **1 quiz**, OR  
2. **1 verified support action**, OR  
3. **1 monthly update**  

If **none** completed → **inactivity penalty (−1%)**.

---

## 10.2 Maximum Monthly Activity Limit

To ensure the program is **not a full-time job**, and stays aligned with Sinegual’s life philosophy, activity is **capped**:

**Maximum allowed per month:**

- **4** verified support actions (1 per week average)  
- **1** quiz  
- **1** major update  
- **Maximum +0.5%** growth for the month  

Extra contributions are **allowed** but do **not** increase percentage further.

# 7. Maintaining the Percentage

Once earned, percentages are **not permanent**. Affiliates must remain active by completing **monthly tasks**.

## Monthly Maintenance Tasks

Complete **at least one** of:

1. **1 quiz** OR  
2. **1 verified support action** OR  
3. **1 update participation**  

If **none** are completed:

- **−1%** inactivity penalty  
- (up to **−5%** total)
